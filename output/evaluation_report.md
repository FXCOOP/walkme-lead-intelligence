## Where the System Breaks

### Known Failure Modes

1. **Personal email + senior title**: The system correctly flags these as low data confidence, 
   but the deterministic scorer cannot verify whether the claim is real. These leads get 
   appropriately routed to human review, but a production system needs email verification.

2. **Event badge scans with no digital engagement**: Leads like L019 and L028 have zero time 
   on site because their interaction was physical. The engagement score underweights them. 
   Fix: add an event-source bypass that overrides time-on-site for badge scan leads.

3. **[FIXED]** Partnership inquiries now route to Partnership Team. partner_inquiry was removed 
   from high_intent_forms (0 intent points), and routing.py overrides routing to 
   Partnership Team / Channel Manager regardless of tier score.

4. **Junior titles at massive companies**: L020 (PwC Senior Associate) and L030 (GM Analyst) 
   get deprioritized by title, but at 300K+ employee companies, junior champions can drive 
   enterprise deals. Fix: add a company-size override that boosts champion-path leads.

5. **[FIXED]** Country abbreviations no longer cause silent scoring losses. A COUNTRY_ALIASES dict 
   in config.py maps common abbreviations (US, UK, UAE, etc.) to full names. scoring.py 
   normalizes country before geography lookup. L020 (PwC, country='US') now gets correct 
   tier-1 geography points.

6. **Geographic bias**: The model penalizes tier-2 geographies (India, Brazil, UAE) even when 
   the lead profile is otherwise enterprise-grade. This reflects a Western-centric bias in 
   the ICP definition that may not match actual revenue potential.

### What to Fix First

1. **Email verification integration** — highest-impact data quality improvement
2. **Event-source engagement bypass** — prevents false negatives from offline leads
3. **Partnership routing path** — prevents misrouting of non-buyer leads
